# Praeco

Terms for governed financial work and the evidence associated with it.

## Language

**Payable Case**:
A tenant's workspace linking documents, messages and accounting references about a possible financial obligation.
_Avoid_: Using the case ID as the obligation's duplicate identity.

**Assignment**:
A durable responsibility to investigate or process a Payable Case, with an operational outcome distinct from a financial verdict.
_Avoid_: Treating a message, specialist persona or execution attempt as the Assignment.

**Agent Principal**:
The stable tenant-bound service identity representing Praeco.
_Avoid_: A separate financial identity for each specialist persona.

**Specialist Role**:
Vera, Kael or Nova's bounded responsibility within Praeco's work.
_Avoid_: Treating a role as a grant of financial authority.

**Approval Request**:
A bounded request for human consent to a particular eligible financial intent.
_Avoid_: A chat reply or an unlimited permission increase.

**Approval Wait**:
Operational work awaiting the authoritative resolution of an Approval Request.
_Avoid_: A budget reservation or financial approval.

**Approval Resolution**:
The authoritative lifecycle result of an Approval Request, with any resulting financial decision recorded separately from its original pending decision.
_Avoid_: Assuming approval means the financial obligation was authorized.

**Financial Decision**:
An authoritative L2 verdict: `AUTHORIZED_SIMULATED`, `BLOCKED` or `PENDING_HUMAN_APPROVAL`.
_Avoid_: `PAID`, an operational Assignment state or a model's explanation as a financial verdict.
